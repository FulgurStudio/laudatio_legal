LAUDATIO — PRIVACY POLICY

Last updated: September 1, 2026

1. Introduction

Welcome to Laudatio, an application developed by Fulgur Studio, an independent developer based in France.

This Privacy Policy explains how Laudatio collects, uses, stores, and protects personal information when you use the app. We are committed to respecting your privacy and collecting only the information reasonably necessary to provide the features you choose to use.

Laudatio does not require you to provide personal information to access the app in Guest Mode. Most profile and spiritual information is optional and provided voluntarily by you.

2. Data Controller

The data controller responsible for processing the personal data described in this Policy is:

Fulgur Studio
Independent Developer — France
Privacy Contact: laudatioapp@gmail.com

3. Information We May Collect

3.1 Account Information

When you create or use an account (e.g., via Google Sign-In), we may receive your email address, account ID, name, and profile picture. Guest Mode requires no personal data.

3.2 Profile & Sacramental Information (Optional)

You may voluntarily provide your name, bio, patron saint, and sacramental dates (Baptism, Confirmation, etc.). This special category data is processed only with your explicit consent and can be deleted at any time.

3.3 Spiritual Progress & Virtual Candles

We store your completed prayers, stats, and Virtual Candles usage. Public Virtual Candle intentions are strictly pre-defined by the app (no free-text entry).

3.4 Artificial Intelligence (Google Gemini)

Laudatio uses Google Gemini for AI assistance (Lectio Divina, spiritual assessments, catechism queries). Only strictly necessary prompts are transmitted under strict data minimization principles.

3.5 Spiritual Diary (100% Private & Local)

The content of your Spiritual Diary is stored exclusively on your local device. It is never transmitted to Laudatio, Supabase, Google Gemini, or any third party.

4. Third-Party Services

We rely on trusted infrastructure providers: Google Play Services (Auth & Billing), Google Gemini (AI), Google AdMob (Rewarded Ads), and Supabase (Secure Database for accounts/stats, excluding the Spiritual Diary).

5. Account Deletion & Rights

You can delete your account inside the app or via our Web Account Deletion Page. Under GDPR, you have full rights to access, correct, or erase your data by contacting us at laudatioapp@gmail.com.
