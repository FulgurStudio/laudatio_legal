Last updated: September 1, 2026

1. Introduction

Welcome to Laudatio, an application developed by Fulgur Studio, an independent developer based in France.

This Privacy Policy explains how Laudatio collects, uses, stores, and protects personal information when you use the app. We are committed to respecting your privacy and collecting only the information reasonably necessary to provide the features you choose to use.

Laudatio does not require you to provide personal information to access the app in Guest Mode. Most profile and spiritual information is optional and provided voluntarily by you.

By using Laudatio, you acknowledge this Privacy Policy and agree to the processing of data as described herein.

2. Data Controller

The data controller responsible for processing the personal data described in this Policy is:

Fulgur Studio
Independent Developer — France
Privacy Contact: laudatioapp@gmail.com

For any privacy-related questions or requests, you may contact us using the email address above.

3. Information We May Collect

Depending on the features you choose to use, Laudatio may process the following categories of information:

3.1 Account Information

When you create or use an account in Laudatio (for example, through Google Sign-In), we may receive:

  • Email address;
  • Account identifier;
  • Name;
  • Profile picture (when provided);
  • Information required for authentication.

In Guest Mode, no personal information is required to browse the application.

3.2 Profile Information (Optional)

You may voluntarily provide:

  • Name and biography;
  • Photo or avatar;
  • Chosen patron saint;
  • Interface and privacy preferences.

These fields are entirely optional.

3.3 Sacramental Information (Optional)

Laudatio allows you to record important dates in your life of faith (such as Baptism, First Communion, Confirmation, and Marriage). This information may reveal or suggest your religious beliefs (considered special category data under GDPR). We process this data only with your explicit consent when you voluntarily enter it. You may choose not to provide this information, and you can delete it at any time.

3.4 Spiritual Progress and Activity

To display your personal history and statistics, we store in your account:

  • Completed prayers, rosaries, and spiritual readings;
  • Experience points, levels, titles, and achievements;
  • Usage statistics for the online Virtual Candles feature.

3.5 Online Virtual Candles

In the Virtual Candles feature, you can light candles with three intention options:

  1. Public intention: Selected exclusively from pre-defined options provided directly by the app (e.g., "For world peace", "For the health of the sick");
  2. Private intention: Associated only with your personal account, with no display of the reason to other users;
  3. No intention: Lighting the candle without indicating a reason.

The app does not contain an open text entry field for public Virtual Candles.

3.6 Profile Pictures

If you upload a photo from your device for your profile, it will be securely stored in Laudatio’s infrastructure to be displayed on your account.

3.7 Artificial Intelligence Features

Laudatio uses Google Gemini to provide AI-assisted responses. Only the information strictly necessary to answer your request is transmitted (data minimization principle). Examples of AI usage include:

  • Suggestions for Lectio Divina and spiritual reflections;
  • Answers concerning the Catechism, Canon Law, Liturgy, and Church Fathers;
  • Analysis of self-knowledge and spiritual assessments;
  • Generation of educational text and images.

3.8 Spiritual Diary (100% Private and Local)

The content of your Spiritual Diary is stored exclusively on your device. No text written in the Spiritual Diary is transmitted to Laudatio servers, Supabase, Google Gemini, or any third party. Laudatio does not read, access, or store your personal diary entries.

3.9 AI Usage Limits

We may maintain technical logs associated with your account to enforce usage limits on AI features and prevent abuse or excessive automated requests.

4. How We Use Information

We use your information to:

  • Create and manage your account;
  • Provide your history, statistics, and spiritual progress;
  • Process AI features and personalize the app;
  • Display rewarded ads (AdMob);
  • Process digital purchases via Google Play Billing;
  • Maintain application security and prevent fraud;
  • Comply with legal obligations.

We never sell your personal information.

5. Legal Bases (GDPR)

We process your data based on:

  • Performance of the service requested by you;
  • Explicit consent (for optional profile/sacramental data);
  • Legitimate interest in keeping the application secure and functional;
  • Legal compliance.

You may withdraw your consent at any time without affecting the lawfulness of processing carried out prior to withdrawal.

6. Google Sign-In

If you choose to log in with Google, Google will provide us with your email address, name, and profile picture for authentication purposes. Laudatio does not receive access to your Google account beyond what is necessary to sign you in.

7. Third-Party Services

To operate seamlessly, Laudatio uses the following third-party service providers:

  • Google Play Services: Authentication and purchases;
  • Google Gemini: Artificial intelligence;
  • Google AdMob: Rewarded advertising;
  • Supabase: Secure database for profile storage and progress (excluding the Spiritual Diary).

8. Storage in Supabase

We use Supabase infrastructure to store your account data (email, name, achievements, prayer history, and Virtual Candles). We reinforce: your Spiritual Diary is not transmitted to or stored in Supabase.

9. Advertising (Google AdMob)

Laudatio may display rewarded ads via Google AdMob (e.g., voluntarily watching a video to unlock a virtual candle). Where required by applicable law, we will obtain your consent before displaying personalized ads. Watching ads is never mandatory to use the basic application features.

10. Purchases and Contributions

All digital purchases or voluntary contributions made in the app are processed exclusively through Google Play's official billing system (Google Play Billing). Laudatio does not directly store or process your banking or credit card details.

11. Data Retention

  • Account and Profile Data: Retained as long as your account remains active.
  • Progress: Retained while the account exists to provide your charts and achievements.
  • Spiritual Diary: Stored locally on your device only. If you uninstall the app or clear app data on your device, the diary entries will be deleted.

12. Account and Data Deletion

You can delete your account and all associated personal data at any time:

  1. In-App: In your profile settings section.
  2. Via Web: Through the link https://fulgurstudio.github.io/laudatio_legal/delete-account.html.

13. User Rights (GDPR)

When applicable, you have the right to access, correct, or request deletion of your personal data, restrict processing, withdraw consent, or lodge a complaint with a competent authority (in France, the CNIL).

To exercise your rights, please contact us via email: laudatioapp@gmail.com.

14. Children's Privacy

Laudatio is not specifically directed to children and does not knowingly collect personal information from children for independent account creation. Guest Mode requires no personal data.

15. International Transfers

As Fulgur Studio is based in France and uses global service providers (such as Google), your data may be processed on servers located outside your country of residence under appropriate legal safeguards.

16. Security

We implement reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect your personal information. Users are also responsible for safeguarding their own devices and credentials.

17. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically. Updates will be posted on this page with the revised date indicated at the top of the document.
